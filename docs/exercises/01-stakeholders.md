# Exercise 01: Stakeholder Identification

## Assignment

Who are the stakeholders of the SpeedLimit project?

## Existing Evidence

Stakeholders identified: the highway operator, traffic/safety
authority, data owner, environmental expert or meteorologist, driver
representative, and project team. They also call out legal/regulatory and
IT/security representatives as needed. The document contains questions for a
proposed requirements meeting and clearly states no real meeting or
stakeholder answers are claimed.

## LLMs response

Additional groups an LLM-assisted answer could add to the manual list above:

- **Sensor-maintenance staff:** maintenance access, calibration, and fast
 detection of faulty or missing sensors.
- **Emergency services:** timely information about incidents, hazardous
 conditions, and system failures that affect their response.
- **Road-maintenance teams (e.g. winter service):** knowing when black-ice
 warnings and speed reductions are active to plan gritting and repairs.
- **Data-protection officer:** data minimization, retention, and lawful
 handling of accident records and licence plates.
- **Passengers:** affected by speed changes and safety like drivers, but
 without control over the vehicle.

This is a proposed LLM-style expansion, not a record of an actual model call.
