# Exercise 02: Legal and Regulatory Compliance

## Assignment

Is the Speed Limit System compliant with relevant norms if it is deployed
everywhere in the world? Choose whether it is compliant, not compliant, or
compliant only with technical and organizational measures, and explain.

## Existing Evidence

The system cannot be assumed compliant for deployment everywhere in the
world. Traffic laws, speed ranges, road-control rules, privacy requirements,
cybersecurity obligations, and rules for AI systems differ between
jurisdictions. The project does not define one deployment jurisdiction and
does not have a legal or safety certification.

Before a deployment could be assessed, the operator would need to select the
jurisdiction and have qualified specialists review at least:

- permitted speed limits and authority to change signs;
- road-safety approval, hazard analysis, and independent validation;
- privacy and data-protection requirements, including data retention and
  access control;
- cybersecurity, resilience, incident reporting, and update procedures;
- applicable AI, product, and public-infrastructure regulations;
- organizational responsibilities, operator training, and audit procedures.

Technical and organizational measures may be necessary, but their existence
would not by itself prove compliance. The current software is a course
simulation, not an approved system for controlling public-road signs. This is
an academic analysis, not legal advice.
